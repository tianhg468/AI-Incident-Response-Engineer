## Instructions

Create a thorough post-mortem that includes:

1. **Summary**: Brief overview of what happened
2. **Timeline**: Chronological sequence of key events
3. **Root Cause**: Detailed explanation of what went wrong and why
4. **Contributing Factors**: Other factors that enabled or worsened the incident
5. **Remediation**: What was done to resolve the incident
6. **Action Items**: Specific, actionable steps to prevent recurrence

Write in clear, blame-free language. Focus on:
- What we learned
- How to prevent this in the future
- System improvements needed
- Monitoring/alerting gaps

Format as markdown suitable for:
- Slack posting
- Saving to incident documentation
- Team review
